# Week 25 Assessment

> **The assessment is the mock interviews.** Recordings, not a quiz.

## The checklist
- [ ] 8 STAR stories written, each **timed at 90 seconds**
- [ ] The 30-second and 5-minute versions of each project
- [ ] The ten memorized numbers
- [ ] 3 system designs, recorded, reviewed
- [ ] Bash, Python, and Terraform exercises practiced under time
- [ ] 5 more broken-cluster scenarios, under 15 minutes each, narrated
- [ ] **6 mock interviews, at least 3 with real engineers**
- [ ] Every mock recorded and watched back
- [ ] The experience question answered without hesitation
- [ ] Your questions for them, written down
- [ ] 20+ applications sent

## Self-review — score each recording

| Signal | Target |
|---|---|
| Silences over 5 seconds | **0** |
| STAR stories over 2 minutes | 0 |
| Specific numbers used per answer | ≥ 1 |
| Trade-offs named per design answer | ≥ 3 |
| "I don't know, but here's how I'd find out" used when true | **at least once** |
| Answers ended deliberately | every time |

That fifth row matters. A candidate who never says it is either being asked only easy questions or is
bluffing — and interviewers can usually tell which.

## Part A — Rapid fire (say these out loud, 20 min)

You should answer each in under 90 seconds without preparation:

1. Walk me through what happens when you type a URL and hit enter.
2. What makes an AWS subnet public?
3. Security groups vs NACLs.
4. RDS Multi-AZ vs read replicas.
5. `count` vs `for_each` in Terraform.
6. What's in Terraform state and why does it matter?
7. A pod is in CrashLoopBackOff. Walk me through it.
8. requests vs limits — what happens when you exceed each?
9. Liveness vs readiness, and the disaster from getting it wrong.
10. Explain IRSA.
11. Why OIDC instead of access keys in CI?
12. What's an SBOM and why generate one?
13. A critical CVE blocks your release and there's no patch. What do you do?
14. What are the four golden signals and how would you alert on them?
15. Explain a golden path in 60 seconds.
16. Tell me about a mistake you made.
17. You don't have professional experience in this.
18. Why the career change?
19. Where do you want to be in three years?
20. What questions do you have for me?

**All twenty are answered in [`../../_shared/interview-bank/`](../_shared/interview-bank/).** Any you
can't answer fluently is this weekend's work.

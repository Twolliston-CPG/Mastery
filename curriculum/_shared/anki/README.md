# Anki Decks

Import the `.tsv` files into Anki: **File → Import**, field separator **Tab**, type **Basic**,
first field = Front, second = Back, third = Tags.

## Rules

1. **Write your own cards as you go.** These are a starter set. Writing a card *is* most of the
   learning — the act of compressing a concept into a question is the retrieval practice.
2. **One fact per card.** If the answer has three parts, make three cards.
3. **Make cards from your mistakes.** Every wrong practice-exam answer and every break/fix scenario
   becomes a card the same day.
4. **Review daily, 15–20 min.** Missing days is what kills spaced repetition; the backlog compounds
   and then you quit.

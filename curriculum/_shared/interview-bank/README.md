## How to use

1. **Cover the answer.** Say yours out loud. Time it — most answers should be 60–120 seconds.
2. **Compare**, don't memorize. The model answers show *shape and depth*, not words to recite.
   A recited answer is obvious and worse than a rough honest one.
3. **Record yourself** once a week. You will hear filler words, rambling, and trailing off. Nothing
   else surfaces those as fast.
4. **"I don't know, but here's how I'd find out" is a passing answer.** Silence is not. Practice
   saying it without apology.

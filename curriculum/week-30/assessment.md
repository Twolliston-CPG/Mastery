## Ten rapid-fire questions (Thursday evening, out loud, under a minute each)

1. The per-task ritual, six steps, in order.
2. The four troubleshooting families and the first command for each.
3. Pending with no events: which component, and why no other cause looks like that.
4. The three etcdctl cert flags and where to copy them from in ten seconds.
5. The upgrade order for a worker node, from drain to uncordon.
6. Why a RoleBinding to a ClusterRole on `nodes` grants nothing.
7. Default-deny in a namespace: the one policy that must ship with it, and why.
8. `Released` versus `Available` on a PV: what is in the way, and the one patch that clears it.
9. The two-minute rule, and what happens at 1:50.
10. The three checklist items that cost whole attempts, not minutes.
